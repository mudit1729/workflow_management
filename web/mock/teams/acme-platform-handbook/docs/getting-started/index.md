# Getting started

Your first week on the platform team, in the order that works.

## The first week

1. **Day 1.** Set up your laptop: [[getting-started/local-setup]].
2. **Days 2 and 3.** Ship a small change to staging: [[getting-started/first-deploy]].
3. **Day 5.** Sit in on the on-call handover and read the [runbooks](../runbooks/index.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Nobody expects you to know the services yet. [[architecture/service-topology]] is the map to keep open in a tab.
